# Scale figure

`Xbot.glb` — Mixamo character with `walk` / `idle` animation (Adobe Mixamo), taken from the three.js
examples repository (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf).
The viewer re-colours it plain matt white.

Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free for use in personal and commercial projects,
but may not be redistributed as standalone assets. It is embedded here only as a scale figure.
